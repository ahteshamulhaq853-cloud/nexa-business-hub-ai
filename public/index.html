Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful information about using Nexa Business Hub AI.

Can I receive AI responses in Urdu?

Yes. Open the AI Assistant and choose Urdu or Roman Urdu from the response language menu. English is the default option.

Is the website interface available in English?

Yes. Navigation, buttons, service names, instructions and the website interface are in English.

Can I save an AI response as a PDF?

Yes. Generate a response, select Print / PDF, and choose Save as PDF in your browser's print dialog.

Will AI responses always be accurate?

AI can make mistakes. Review important information and verify legal, medical, financial or other high-impact decisions with reliable sources or qualified professionals.
